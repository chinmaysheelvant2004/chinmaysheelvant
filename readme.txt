CHINMAY — PERSONAL WEBSITE

Your website is in index.html.

FREE PUBLISHING WITH NETLIFY:
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Sign in or create a free Netlify account.
3. Drag the chinmay_personal_website folder (or the ZIP file) onto the Netlify Drop page.
4. Netlify will publish the website and give you a public URL.
5. You can share that URL. You do not need to buy a domain or hosting.

To update the website later, edit index.html and upload the updated folder again.

Before publishing, review the wording and public contact details. Anything on this website will be visible to visitors.
